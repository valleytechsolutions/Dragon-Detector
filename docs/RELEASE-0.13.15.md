# Dragon Detector 0.13.15

- Adds Capsule Corp on all five boards: a silver Dragon Radar bezel, emerald grid, yellow detected-signal markers and the supplied Capsule Corp emblem.
- Adds the Capsule Corp chronometer with a white-and-blue analog dial, green digital time, date and animated amber seconds. The complete watch design also runs on Waveshare Lite.
- Adds Sparrows to the locksport collection: black/red/steel radar, keyhole emblem, two pick-shaped watch hands and a Locksport Timer with a moving rim. The full watch also runs on Lite.
- Adds matching light menus, settings and dialogs on full boards. There are now 33 themes and 31 independently selectable full-board watch faces.
- The web flasher includes both new themes and checks firmware compatibility before selecting it. Existing saved themes, McNally logo placement and the Dragon Ball/BWM boot remain unchanged.
