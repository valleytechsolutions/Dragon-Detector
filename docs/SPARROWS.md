# Sparrows

Firmware **0.13.15** adds Sparrows to the locksport collection on all five boards. Select **Sparrows** in Settings → Theme & radar labels or the web flasher. Lite cycles themes with BOOT. USB: `radar theme sparrows` (ID 32); full-board watch ID 35.

The supplied red keyhole emblem and distressed white wordmark sit on black. Steel markers, broken red rings and textured pick handles extend that style through the radar, watch, menus and dialogs. The radar's hook sweep is decorative; contact positions continue to represent signal strength, not measured direction or distance.

The watch has two stylized steel picks for hands, an amber-red seconds marker, digital local time and a date. The full watch design also runs on Lite. Selecting the theme changes Stopwatch to **Locksport Timer** with START/PAUSE, SPLIT, OPEN and a confirmed RESET. The rim indicator moves while counting and freezes when paused or opened. Lite offers the same elapsed-time theme with its compact button controls; see [Lite controls](WAVESHARE-LITE.md).

## Design references and artwork

Sparrows' official [Black Flag](https://www.sparrowslockpicks.com/products/black-flag-picks) design uses a geometric grip, steel core and red keyhole orientation mark. Its [VENNOX](https://www.sparrowslockpicks.com/products/draft-new-lock-pick-kit) set pairs dark styling with engraved artwork. Those references informed the black/red/steel palette and engraved dial details; the firmware picks are stylized drawings, not exact product replicas.

The user-supplied [`sparrows-source.jpg`](../media/theme-logos/sparrows-source.jpg) is preserved unchanged, including its black background, and encoded directly for the black display surface.
