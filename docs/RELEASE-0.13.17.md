# Dragon Detector 0.13.17 — Personal themes and DZ_AZ

[Install firmware](https://valleytech-dragon-detector.pages.dev/#install) · [Firmware files and checksums](https://valleytech-dragon-detector.pages.dev/guides/DOWNLOADS.html) · [Hosting status](HOSTING-STATUS.md)

Create one personal clock, radar and counter in the web flasher's **Custom theme** tab. Upload a logo, adjust its crop and background, choose colors, clock hands and numerals, and preview all three screens. Select **Install this personal theme** to include it after flashing, or install it directly on current firmware.

- The personal theme stays hidden until explicitly installed. Matching updates preserve it; export/import and USB readback let you keep a portable copy.
- Analog, hybrid and digital layouts; classic, baton, arrow and lockpick hands; three numeral styles; optional date, seconds and locksport controls.
- Transfers are checked before replacing a saved design. Invalid or interrupted uploads keep the previous theme.
- DZ_AZ adds a crow-and-name emblem, rotating feather radar sweep, and short/long feather clock hands in charcoal and cool silver.
- Available on Waveshare AMOLED 1.75C, M5Stack StopWatch, Spotpear, Seeed XIAO Round and Waveshare Lite. The collection now has 34 built-in themes, plus the optional personal slot.

Clock/counter scanning pauses, automatic USB time setup and existing saved settings remain supported. Use the matching **Update** image to preserve files and settings.
