# Dragon Detector 0.13.12

Hackazillarex and m33k_t3ch bring the catalog to **31 themes and 29 selectable logo watches** on the four full boards. Both new themes also include animated compact clocks on Waveshare Lite.

- **Hackazillarex:** black, red and ivory throughout the interface; a rotating bone replaces the radar line. The supplied logo fills the watch background while an original pixel T-rex runs and jumps over approaching cacti. Time remains above the animation.
- **m33k_t3ch:** navy, cyan, magenta and ivory; paired neon radar beams, a circular robot logo, digital time, a second-by-second rim and animated circuit lights. The circular artwork was prepared from the supplied screenshot; the original is retained and the generated derivative is credited.
- Menus, dialogs and notices use each selected theme. Existing saved theme/watch IDs remain stable. Lite keeps BOOT tap/hold controls and all five pages.
- USB clock setup, separate Time/Date/Sync tabs, hardware RTC readback, optional saved-Wi-Fi recovery and the universal VTS startup remain available. [Clock setup](CLOCK-SETUP.md).

The **21% application-slot reserve guard remains unchanged** on every profile. Both new themes use native-size compressed assets and allocation-free animation. Build/package checks cover all five boards; connected-device evidence and measured budgets are recorded in [validation](VALIDATION.md) and [flash budget](FLASH-BUDGET.md). Lite hardware testing remains paused at the owner's request.
