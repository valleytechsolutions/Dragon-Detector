# Dragon Detector 0.13.18

Can Commander joins the theme collection: a tribute to Matthew Kukanich with the original MK logo, navy and silver panels, blue differential-signal traces and ECU-style circuit details.

- CAN-inspired radar with paired sweep traces and animated perimeter packets.
- Dedicated clock with hour/minute/second fields, date, seconds marker and a hexadecimal clock-data readout.
- Matching session stopwatch with an elapsed-time perimeter indicator, menus and dialogs.
- Available on Waveshare AMOLED, M5Stack StopWatch, Spotpear, Seeed XIAO Round and Waveshare Lite.

There are now 35 built-in radar themes plus the optional personal theme. Existing selections and personal-theme IDs are preserved. CAN visuals are decorative; Wi-Fi/Bluetooth detection and clock/timer power saving are unchanged.
