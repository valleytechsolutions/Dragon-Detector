Dragon Detector

StopWatch badge importer · images stay on this computer and your connected device.

Connect the StopWatch over USB. Choose a picture, preview its circular crop, then import it. Yellow returns to radar; blue opens Tools. In Badge mode, tap to advance or hold to toggle the slideshow.


Use Chrome or Edge. If USB access is unavailable here, serve this folder locally with python -m http.server 8000, then open http://localhost:8000/Badge-Importer.html.
